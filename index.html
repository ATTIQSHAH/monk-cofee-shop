Local Artists & Community Programs

Every month we rotate a new gallery of pieces from Abilene creators. We host a weekly open‑mic night and partner with the Abilene Mental Health Alliance on a quarterly merch line that raises $5,000 for community programs.

  • Rotating gallery – new local artist every 30 days
  • Weekly open‑mic – every Thursday 7pm
  • Mental‑health merch – 20% of sales go to the Abilene Mental Health Alliance
  • Artist‑in‑Residence program – two creators per quarter
